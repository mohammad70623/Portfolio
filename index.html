About Me

I'm an AI & ML Engineer currently studying Software Engineering at Daffodil International University. Over the past couple of years I've gone deep into machine learning, deep learning, and — more recently — the world of large language models and agentic AI.

Most of my work these days sits at the intersection of LLMs and real-world applications: building RAG systems that answer questions from custom documents, designing multi-step LangGraph agents, and wrapping everything in clean, usable interfaces with Streamlit and FastAPI.

Before that I spent a lot of time on the fundamentals — training CNNs for image classification, working through NLP pipelines, and building predictive models for things like fraud detection and house prices. That foundation still shows up in how I think about problems.

I like building things that actually run. Not just notebooks — deployed apps with real inputs and real outputs.
